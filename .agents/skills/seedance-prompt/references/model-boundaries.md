# Model And Operation Boundaries

Creative intent is reusable across Seedance models. Execution facts are not.
Keep the following authorities separate:

| Authority | Controls |
| --- | --- |
| Current brief and explicit selections | Subject, actions, medium, language, timing, reference authority and preservation locks |
| Selected exact model | Model identity, not a permission or inferred feature set |
| Verified operation profile | Exact provider, model, operation, transport and tool-schema revision, with provenance and validity |
| Workspace policy | Generation scope, rights, approval/review depth, preview and persistence requirements |
| Actual output evidence | Observed image, motion, voice, stream metadata and final acceptance |

## Settings

Prompt-only handoff can remain useful while execution controls are unresolved.
Return text and separate verified or unresolved settings without registry writes,
media creation, tool discovery against credentials or production initialization.

Unknown support remains unresolved. A model catalog entry or local documentation
alone does not establish account activation, connected-tool support or an
operation's actual schema. Do not transfer standard-model facts to Flash, Fast,
Mini, Premium or a future variant. Generic `Seedance` is not an API model ID.
Preserve selected exact identifiers and explicit locked delivery targets.

Verified parameter and reference limits apply to the selected operation and
destination. Image/video/audio role totals, frame/reference combinations,
duration bounds, source constraints and encoding choices may differ. Keep
unsupported fields out of execution settings and do not silently omit a
required field to fit a transport.

## Preview And Promotion

Native Draft, an ordinary preview and a final target are different stages.
Preserve applicable mandatory native-Draft policy for existing exact standard
bindings. A missing or conflicting capability is a stop for execution, not
permission to substitute ordinary 480p or change models.

A verified non-native operation may use its supported ordinary preview under
the caller's policy; label it non-promotable. A new or unknown model cannot
inherit Draft or promotion because its name resembles a previous version.
Promotion requires verified mechanics and the exact selected successful native
task, current scope, unchanged creative inputs, profile/transport compatibility
and valid expiry. Inspect the final artifact again; selection of a preview is
not final approval.

## History And Proposals

Preserve historical exact IDs, task identities, prompt/reference/request hashes
and decisions. A new profile cannot rewrite a submitted operation or convert an
ordinary historical task into native Draft. Reconcile ambiguous submissions
before retrying or changing transport.

Keep alternative models, preview workflows, separate audio, derivative sources
and extra probes unselected proposals until authorized. Preserve source
duration, frame timing and audio tails; authoring intent is not permission to
trim, round, pad, retime, mute or generate.

Real-person consent, face asset authorization and voice asset authorization are
independent. Planning boards stay ineligible in every conditioning role. Neither
creative approval nor a model choice waives these boundaries.

# Anamorphic Treatment

Adapted from the user-supplied `Anamorphic skill.md` associated with the
Higgsfield workflow. Use visible optical targets rather than a brand or a
bare "cinematic" label. These are creative heuristics, not measured provider
behavior. No downloaded template or external source library is redistributed.

## Resolve the shot, not a fixed package

1. Preserve medium, camera position, framing, focus, lighting, grade and
   delivery locks. Anamorphic is not automatically photorealistic or 2.39:1.
2. State the shot's job and current geometry. For a new unlocked setup,
   propose focal intent appropriate to geography, group, performance or
   detail. Do not invent a focal length when the request asks only for lens
   character. A 24mm close face can exaggerate proportions at close distance;
   this is not a ban on wide lenses or people in establishing shots.
3. If blur is requested, name the sharp subject/plane and the layers allowed
   to soften. Camera distance, background separation and aperture may be
   proposed for an unlocked setup, not imposed on established blocking.
   Neither "wide open" nor a focal number guarantees generated blur.
4. If bokeh is requested and defocused highlights exist, describe **vertically
   elongated oval highlights** in those named areas. An object in sharp focus
   does not become an oval simply because the lens is anamorphic.
5. If a flare is requested, name its motivating source and a **horizontal
   streak**. If the scene has no suitable source, return the conflict or
   propose adding one for approval. Do not silently add a lamp or sun.
6. Keep grain, film stock, halation, haze, weather and color grade separate.
   Add them only when requested or approved. Daylight/tungsten stock vocabulary
   is a look choice, not a required tail or an exposure control.
7. Return one compact optical phrase, any unresolved conflicts, and output
   requirements separately. The caller resolves supported parameters.

## Optical and delivery distinctions

- Focal length does not have a universal field of view. Format/sensor
  dimensions, squeeze, crop and horizontal/vertical/diagonal convention
  matter. Do not copy the source's fixed millimetre-to-degree table or
  replace a requested focal length with an assumed field of view.
- The source color does not alone determine flare color; optical coatings
  and the selected look matter. A requested blue streak from a warm lamp is
  not inherently a physical error. Do not impose orange as a correction.
- Anamorphic does not mandate a diagonal three-quarter camera, shallow focus
  or a flare in every frame. Preserve deep focus, frontal framing and clean
  optical treatments when locked.
- `21:9` equals approximately `2.333:1`, not `2.39:1`. Do not substitute them
  as exact equivalents. Keep native generation aspect and delivery crop
  separate; propose a supported canvas and explicit framing-safe finishing
  path for approval if exact delivery is unavailable. Do not bake bars into
  the generated image or claim prose sets encoded dimensions.
- `4K` is a capability/delivery requirement, not a lens effect. Preserve the
  selected model and surface unsupported output without automatic switching.

## Compact examples

All examples are hypothetical. They establish requested looks, not results.

**Approved shallow-focus night setting with a practical in view:**

> Anamorphic character; the near subject stays sharp while the established
> defocused background bulbs form vertical oval highlights. The visible
> practical carries the requested restrained horizontal streak.

Do not apply this sentence if the brief did not request blur and flare.

**Locked deep-focus daylight courtyard, no flare:**

> Restrained rectilinear anamorphic character, preserving the frontal
> framing and near-to-far detail.

There may be little distinctive anamorphic evidence in that setup. Say so
instead of violating the focus or lighting lock to make the label obvious.

**Requested wide establishing shot with stronger subject separation:**

> Keep the approved wide composition and camera position; hold the named
> foreground subject sharp and soften only the approved far-background layer.

If that conflicts with a focus lock, flag it. A camera relocation or new
foreground object is a separate proposal, not a lens correction.

## Review

Check the requested sharp plane, permitted soft areas, bokeh orientation when
visible, flare source/orientation when present, intact subject proportions,
medium and camera continuity. Inspect actual dimensions independently of
the look. No mandatory stock grain, extra light, haze, face close-up, fixed
FOV, 2.39 crop or photorealism should appear merely from selecting anamorphic.

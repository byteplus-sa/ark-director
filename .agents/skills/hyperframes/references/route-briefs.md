# Route briefs (moved)

Each route's interview entry — must-haves, conditional questions, deferred asks,
pitch-eligible fields, and whether the run-shape questions apply — lives in that
workflow's own `SKILL.md`, together with its input/output/trigger contract.
Workflow skills and their route contracts are not vendored in this workspace;
install the matched workflow (`npx hyperframes skills update <workflow-name>`)
and read its `SKILL.md`.

The interview procedure itself (the eight steps) is `references/intent-interview.md`.
Field semantics and question rules: `hyperframes/references/brief-contract.md` § 2–3.

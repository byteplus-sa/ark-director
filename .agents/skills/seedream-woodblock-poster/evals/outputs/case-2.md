# Case 2 — Fox, owl, and whale series

## Visual specification

Three separate vertical, borderless posters on clean cream paper. Cobalt dominates the silhouettes and contour marks; rust supplies limited environmental accents; cream paper supplies light and negative space. The shared treatment is flat carved ink shapes and fine contour hatching, with restrained ink grain. The fox has a long brush tail and pointed ears; the owl has a round facial disk, hooked beak, and feathered wings; the whale has an elongated body, paired pectoral flippers, and a broad horizontal tail fluke.

## Ordered image bindings

None. These are three text-only requests.

## Prompt 1 — Fox

Task: Create an original fox illustrated woodblock art poster.
Subject: A slender fox with pointed ears, a tapered muzzle, exactly four legs, and one long brush tail. It steps diagonally along a hillside, turns its head toward the viewer, and curves its tail behind its body to form a clear sweeping silhouette.
Setting: A rolling grassland hillside with rust seed heads, small cobalt pines, and a winding footpath in the lower portion.
Style: Cobalt, cream, and rust only. Flat carved ink shapes with fine contour hatching, restrained ink grain, and clean cream paper. Printed pigment highlights and paper negative space define form. Borderless artwork with no frame, no aged staining, and consistent medium detail density.
Lighting: Cream paper cuts articulate the face, chest, and tail; restrained rust accents catch the grass tips like stylized evening pigment.
Composition: Tall vertical poster, diagonal fox silhouette in the middle, hillside sweeping upward from the lower left, and generous cream sky. Keep all four feet and the tail tip framed.
Constraints: Opaque clean-paper background and text-free illustration. One fox with readable anatomy, no lettering or seals. Borderless framing.

## Prompt 2 — Owl

Task: Create an original owl illustrated woodblock art poster.
Subject: An owl with a rounded facial disk, two forward-facing eyes, a small hooked beak, exactly two feathered wings, two gripping feet, and a short fan-shaped tail. Perch it upright on a crooked branch, wings folded against its body, face turned directly toward the viewer.
Setting: Sparse woodland branches and a small rust moon behind the owl, with a distant cobalt ridge near the lower edge.
Style: Cobalt, cream, and rust only. Flat carved ink shapes with fine contour hatching, restrained ink grain, and clean cream paper. Printed pigment highlights and paper negative space define form. Borderless artwork with no frame, no aged staining, and consistent medium detail density.
Lighting: Cream paper cuts illuminate the facial disk and layered feathers; rust moon pigment supplies a restrained circular accent behind the head.
Composition: Tall vertical poster, compact frontal owl silhouette high in the composition, offset branch running horizontally, and broad open cream space around the round head. Keep the full head, folded wings, tail, and feet framed.
Constraints: Opaque clean-paper background and text-free illustration. One owl with readable paired wings and feet, no lettering or seals. Borderless framing.

## Prompt 3 — Whale

Task: Create an original whale illustrated woodblock art poster.
Subject: A whale with an elongated streamlined torso, broad rounded head, exactly two pectoral flippers, and a broad horizontal tail fluke. Show the complete animal gliding upward through water in a lateral three-quarter view, the far flipper distinctly readable and the tail flowing down toward the lower right.
Setting: Open ocean rendered as sweeping carved wave currents, a tiny distant rocky island at the surface, and sparse rust kelp near the lower edge.
Style: Cobalt, cream, and rust only. Flat carved ink shapes with fine contour hatching, restrained ink grain, and clean cream paper. Printed pigment highlights and paper negative space define form. Borderless artwork with no frame, no aged staining, and consistent medium detail density.
Lighting: Cream paper cuts trace the head, back, and flippers; rust kelp supplies limited warm accents against cobalt water.
Composition: Tall vertical poster, long ascending whale silhouette across the center, horizontal surface currents above, and open cream water gaps around its body. Keep the full head, both flippers, and complete tail fluke framed.
Constraints: Opaque clean-paper background and text-free illustration. One whale with paired pectoral flippers and horizontal tail fluke, no lettering or seals. Borderless framing.

## Visible-design checklist

- Three separate designs, each independently usable and text-only.
- Exact shared Style block repeated across all three prompts.
- Cobalt, cream, and rust; clean paper; no border or frame.
- Distinct diagonal fox, compact frontal owl, and ascending elongated whale silhouettes.
- Distinct hillside, woodland, and ocean settings.
- Correct readable species anatomy and complete framing at thumbnail size.
- No lettering, seals, invented image references, or glossy modeled surfaces.

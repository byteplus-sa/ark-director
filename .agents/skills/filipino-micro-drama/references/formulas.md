# Formulas

Read [the entrypoint](../SKILL.md). Each formula lists its engine, a
three-scene shape, and the kind of physical proof that makes the reveal read on
a phone screen. Named shows are genre signposts only; never copy their
characters or plots.

| Formula | Engine | Hook → Turn → Cliffhanger | Proof on screen | Genre signposts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kontrabida matriarch / hidden heir | A mistreated helper or spouse is secretly the heir | Accused of theft → sampal, then the lawyer reads the will → the matriarch whispers a deeper secret | Sealed will, empty jewelry box | *Mara Clara*, *Maid for Revenge* |
| Kabit and the legal wife | Wife confronts husband and mistress | Wife finds proof → public confrontation and sampal → mistress produces a counter-proof | Matching ring, photo, land title | *The Legal Wife*, *Linlang* |
| OFW homecoming betrayal | Years of remittances, a surprise return | Loving video call → a stranger opens the door → "the house is in my name" | Balikbayan box, title deed, borrowed daster | *Kung Ako'y Iiwan Mo* |
| Switched at birth | A poor girl is the rich family's real child | Class humiliation → a nurse sees a birthmark and confesses → the winner lies about the result | Birthmark, DNA envelope | *Mara Clara* |
| Revenge under a new identity | The ruined heroine returns as someone else | Glamorous stranger arrives → the enemy recognizes a detail → "you should have checked if I was dead" | Scar, heirloom, old photo | *Lavender Fields* |
| Rich–poor Cinderella | Love across class | Collision in a status space → mother offers money → the poor side refuses, or the rich side follows | Cash envelope, uniform versus designer | *Can't Buy Me Love* |
| CEO fake relationship (kilig) | A contract romance with a hidden agenda | Grabbed as a fake fiancée → a comic slap and a payoff offer → she already knew him | Envelope, a photo on her phone (screen never shown) | *What's Wrong with Secretary Kim* (PH) |
| Barkada / campus | Friendship, rivalry, first love | Public embarrassment → the loyal friend stands up → a betrayal inside the group | Confession letter, phone video (screen never shown) | *Ang Mutya ng Section E* |
| Aswang / folk supernatural | Province, a lola's warnings, night | Garlic at the window → a sleepwalker is slapped awake → a monstrous tell only the audience sees | A winged shadow, a reflection | *Sang'gre* (fantasy), provincial horror |
| True-to-life sacrifice | Poverty, illness, injustice | A small daily struggle → the sacrifice is revealed → the cost lands | Receipt, hospital bill, a packed bag | *Magpakailanman* |

## Choosing for AI production

- Prefer formulas whose proof is a single object or mark that one close-up can
  show.
- Formulas with one location per scene and two or three characters hold
  identity best.
- Kabit and hidden-heir stories are the strongest vertical-platform performers
  in 2024–2026. Aswang and kilig add range to a batch.

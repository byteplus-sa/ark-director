# Episode Brief

Read [the entrypoint](../SKILL.md). Fill every field; a production
orchestrator treats the dialogue as locked wording.

## Template

```text
## <slug> — "<Title>" (<formula>)

Formula: <formula + key devices>.
Logline: <one sentence>.
Cliffhanger question: <the question the episode leaves open>.

Cast (≤3): **<Name>** (<age>, <look>, <wardrobe>, always-worn: <items>). ...
Location(s): <name> — <layout, landmarks, light, time of day>.
Props: <story-critical objects; note "no legible text">.

S1 Hook (<N> s): <action beats>.
- <Speaker>: {<exact line>}
S2 Turn (<N> s): <action, sampal or reveal, proof shown in close-up>.
- <Speaker>: {<exact line>}
S3 Cliffhanger (<N> s): <action>; hold on <character>'s face.
- <Speaker>: {<exact line>}

Subtitles: "<Tagalog>" = "<English>"; ...
Limits: fictional cast, no real brands or legible signage, non-injurious conflict.
```

## Worked example

This brief was produced into a finished 51 s episode in September 2026.

```text
## know-your-place — "Know Your Place" (hidden heiress vs. kontrabida)

Formula: mistreated helper secretly heir + kontrabida matriarch + sampal.
Logline: A young helper accused of theft by the lady of the house learns,
mid-humiliation, that the late Don left everything to her.
Cliffhanger question: Who is Lira's real mother?

Cast: **Lira** (22, helper, low bun, pale-blue uniform with white collar and
apron). **Señora Vivian Montenegro** (55, sleek black hair, cream blazer,
pearls, red lipstick, always-worn: gold bangle). **Atty. Ramos** (62, silver
hair, glasses, cream barong tagalog, brown leather folder).
Location: hacienda foyer — curved narra staircase at left, capiz windows,
chandelier, double front doors at right; afternoon gold light.
Props: open velvet jewelry box with an empty necklace slot; leather folder
with a sealed document (no legible text).

S1 Hook (15 s): Vivian storms down the stairs and hurls the empty box at
Lira's feet; Lira drops to her knees.
- Vivian: {Magnanakaw! Where is it, Lira? Where's my necklace?}
- Lira: {Ma'am, hindi po ako magnanakaw. I swear po, I didn't take it.}
- Vivian: {Know your place, iha. You're just the help.}
S2 Turn (20 s): Vivian slaps Lira; held silence; the doors open and Ramos
enters with the folder.
- Ramos: {Señora, that's enough.}
- Vivian: {Attorney, this girl is a thief.}
- Ramos: {This girl is Don Rafael's daughter. Everything he owned... is hers now.}
S3 Cliffhanger (15 s): Ramos hands Lira the folder; Vivian whispers close.
- Vivian: {Congratulations, iha. But you don't even know who your real mother is.}
Hold on Lira: shock turning to dread.

Subtitles: "Magnanakaw!" = "Thief!"; "hindi po ako magnanakaw" = "I'm not a
thief"; "iha" = "dear (condescending)".
```

Lesson from production: the shouted opening word "Magnanakaw!" was garbled.
Moving the plot-critical Tagalog word mid-sentence at normal volume would have
been safer, for example `{Where is my necklace, magnanakaw?}`.

# Episode Brief

Read [the entrypoint](../SKILL.md). Fill every field; a production
orchestrator treats the dialogue as locked wording.

## Template

```text
## <slug> — "<Title>" (<formula>)

Formula: <formula + key devices>.
Logline: <one sentence>.
Cliffhanger question: <the question the episode leaves open>.

Cast (≤3): **<Name>** (<age>, <complexion and hair>, <appealing look>,
<wardrobe>, always-worn: <items>). ...
Location(s): <name> — <layout, landmarks, light, time of day>.
Props: <story-critical objects, described as plain, unlettered surfaces>.
Dialogue mode: <English-dominant Taglish | English with a Filipino accent>.

S1 Hook (<N> s, <W> words): <action beats>.
- <Speaker>: {<exact line>}
S2 Turn (<N> s, <W> words): <action, sampal or reveal, proof shown in close-up>.
- <Speaker>: {<exact line>}
S3 Cliffhanger (<N> s, <W> words): <action>; hold on <character>'s face.
- <Speaker>: {<exact line>}

Subtitles: "<Tagalog>" = "<English>"; ...
Limits: fictional cast, no real brands, plain unlettered surfaces, non-injurious conflict.
```

Keep each scene's word count near 2.2 words per second of the time the lines
can occupy, with air before the first line and after the last (a 15 s scene
carries roughly 20–25 words).

## Worked example

This brief was produced into a finished 51 s episode in September 2026, then
revised to the current dialogue default: plot words in English, simple Tagalog
for status and warmth.

```text
## know-your-place — "Know Your Place" (hidden heiress vs. kontrabida)

Formula: mistreated helper secretly heir + kontrabida matriarch + sampal.
Logline: A young helper accused of theft by the lady of the house learns,
mid-humiliation, that the late Don left everything to her.
Cliffhanger question: Who is Lira's real mother?

Cast: **Lira** (22, warm brown complexion, bright eyes, low dark bun,
pale-blue uniform with white collar and apron). **Señora Vivian Montenegro**
(55, fair complexion, sleek black bob, elegant and composed, cream blazer,
pearls, red lipstick, always-worn: gold bangle on her left wrist).
**Atty. Ramos** (62, light-brown complexion, kind face, silver hair, glasses,
cream barong tagalog, brown leather folder).
Location: hacienda foyer — curved narra staircase at frame-left, capiz
windows, chandelier, double front doors at frame-right; afternoon gold light
from the capiz windows.
Props: open velvet jewelry box with an empty necklace slot; plain brown leather
folder holding a sealed cream document with a smooth, unmarked face.
Dialogue mode: English-dominant Taglish.

S1 Hook (15 s, 23 words): Vivian storms down the stairs and hurls the empty
box at Lira's feet; Lira drops to her knees.
- Vivian: {Where is it, Lira? Where is my necklace?}
- Lira: {Ma'am, I did not take it po.}
- Vivian: {Know your place, iha. You're just the help.}
S2 Turn (20 s, 22 words): Vivian slaps Lira; held silence; the doors open and
Ramos enters with the folder.
- Ramos: {Señora, that's enough po.}
- Vivian: {Attorney, this girl is a thief.}
- Ramos: {This girl is Don Rafael's daughter. Everything he owned is hers now.}
S3 Cliffhanger (15 s, 12 words): Ramos hands Lira the folder; Vivian whispers
close.
- Vivian: {Congratulations, iha. But you don't even know who your real mother is.}
Hold on Lira: shock turning to dread.

Subtitles: "po" = polite marker (no direct translation); "iha" = "dear
(condescending)".
```

Lesson from production: in the first version the shouted opening word
"Magnanakaw!" was garbled, so the hook landed without its key word. The
revision opens on plain English words at normal volume and keeps Tagalog to
honorifics.

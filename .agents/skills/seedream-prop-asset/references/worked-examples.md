# Worked examples

Hypothetical patterns for `seedream-prop-asset` without bundled result evidence.
Each follows the standard prompt template in `SKILL.md` and the view plan in its
Default production rule section.

## Photoreal front and back sheet: apple

An apple is near-symmetric around its stem axis, so the plan is front and back
with no side view: the back turns the apple half a revolution and shows the
opposite skin pattern. A red apple reads clearly on white, so the default
background applies.

```text
Task:
Text-to-Image (T2I)

Subject:
Prop reference of one ripe apple, about eight centimetres across: a round, slightly tapered apple with a deep red blush streaked over yellow-green skin, a short brown woody stem with one small leaf, and fine pale lenticel dots across the skin. One apple only, stem up, the same apple in both views, identical design. Front: a broad deep-red blush with vertical yellow-green streaks near the stem, the leaf on the right of the stem. Back: the opposite side of the same apple, the red blush fading to a pale yellow-green patch low on the skin with a slightly denser cluster of pale lenticel dots as natural skin pattern, no scar or spot, the leaf now on the left of the stem.

Setting:
Isolated on a pure white seamless background that runs continuously across the whole sheet as a flat even field with no floor line, with a faint contact shadow directly beneath each apple that does not join the next. No other objects.

Style:
Photorealistic product-reference photography, 50mm lens, true skin micro-texture with natural waxy bloom, not CGI-waxy.

Lighting:
Soft, even, neutral studio light, gentle fill, neutral white balance, one soft controlled highlight on the skin, identical across both views.

Composition:
Two views side by side in one horizontal row on one continuous background, separated by empty space only: left, the whole apple in a straight-on front view at eye level; right, the same apple turned half a revolution on a turntable, in a straight-on back view at eye level. Both views drawn at the same scale, the object the same height in each view, each centered with a clear margin of at least 6% of the sheet on every side; same framing and lighting, deep focus across the apple.

Constraints:
Quality: sharp skin detail, clean silhouette, consistent design across both views
Negative: no hands, no knife, no bite, no other fruit, no readable text, no stickers, no labels, no colored or gradient background, no boxes, frames, borders, or divider lines, no off-white rectangles behind the views, no cropped edges, no extra views, no captions, no watermarks
```

## Stylized vehicle sheet: three views

A vehicle's flanks carry its identity, so the plan is front, back, and side; the
long, low body takes the stacked layout on a square canvas. The sidecar flank
carries the most identity, so it is the near flank: the front view and the side
profile show it, and the rear view turns half a revolution to show the other
flank. The tricycle's front end therefore points right of the frame in the front
and side views. Polished chrome trim and a project whose character sheets use
gray call for the gray background.

```text
Task:
Text-to-Image (T2I)

Subject:
Vehicle prop sheet for an original stylized-3D animated family film: one small motorcycle-and-sidecar tricycle, about two metres long. A compact motorcycle with a rounded teal fuel tank and a single round headlight, joined on its right side to a boxy covered sidecar with a curved teal roof, a cream body with a hand-painted marigold-yellow stripe, a padded red vinyl bench seat, and polished chrome grab bars. A small chrome rooster ornament stands on the front of the sidecar roof. The plate holder is empty and blank. Parked upright, headlight off. The same tricycle in all three views, identical design. Front: the round headlight, the front of the sidecar roof with the rooster ornament, a chrome bumper. Back: a single round red tail lamp on the motorcycle, a flat cream rear panel on the sidecar with a small chrome rear step, and a blank plate holder. Side: the full profile from the sidecar side, showing the sidecar's cream body with the marigold stripe, its curved teal roof, a rounded door with a chrome handle, and all three wheels.

Setting:
Neutral light-gray seamless studio background running continuously across the whole sheet as a flat even field with no floor line, with a faint contact shadow directly beneath each view of the tricycle that does not join the next. No people, no other vehicles, no other objects.

Style:
Original stylized-3D family-feature animation vehicle design: rounded appealing proportions, tactile painted metal with a soft clear-coat, mirror-polished chrome, clean animated-feature render quality. Not photoreal, not flat 2D.

Lighting:
Soft, even, neutral studio light, flat fill, neutral white balance, soft controlled chrome highlights, identical across all views.

Composition:
Three views on one continuous background, separated by empty space only. Top row, two views with even spacing: left, the whole tricycle in a front three-quarter view from its front right, from slightly above eye level, the front end at the right of the frame and the sidecar flank on the left; right, the same tricycle turned half a revolution on a turntable, in a rear three-quarter view from its rear left, the rear end at the right of the frame and the left flank on the left. Beneath them, centered: a full side profile of the sidecar flank, square to the vehicle, the front end at the right, with all three wheels fully visible. All three views drawn at the same scale, the tricycle the same height in each view, with a clear margin of at least 6% of the sheet to the canvas edge and to the next view; same lighting.

Constraints:
Quality: square sheet, crisp stylized detail, consistent vehicle design across all views
Negative: no letters, no words, no numbers, no plate characters, no logos, no maker badges, no people, no driver, no other vehicles, no cropped wheels, no boxes, frames, borders, or divider lines, no off-white rectangles behind the views, no cropped edges, no extra views, no captions, no watermarks
```
